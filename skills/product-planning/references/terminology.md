# Terminology

Words used in the brief, register and journeys, each with one meaning. Label a document with the sense of a
word it uses when the word has more than one.

| Term | Meaning | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| Deferred item | Scheduled to a later phase | Exclusion |
| Readiness | Declared checks pass | Human approval of a specific revision |
| Lens | An advisory reviewing perspective | A review by the person it is named for |
