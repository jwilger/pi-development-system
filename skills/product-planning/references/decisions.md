# Decision register

The referential spine: every brief, review and journey cites these ids.

## Closure rules

- **Resolved:** the owner answered or accepted it.
- **Deferred:** postponed to a later phase. State who decided, why, the interim constraint and the revisit
  point. Deferring the specification does not defer the capability; only an explicit scope decision does.
- **Open:** not yet answered.
- Superseding keeps the old row and points at the new one (`D06 → D56`). Rows are never deleted.
