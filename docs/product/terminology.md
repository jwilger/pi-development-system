# Adoption terminology

- **Operational workflow:** project-owned selection, ownership, blocking, delivery and
  completion policy, expressed in version-controlled local guidance and narrow config.
- **Engineering workflow:** devsys's execution/review process and hard stops; not replaced
  or weakened by local operational instructions.
- **Discovery:** evidence-backed survey and owner-approved proposal, not implicit migration.
- **Documented intent:** what local rules say; not proof of current enforcement or success.
- **Observation:** identified source/configuration/API behavior; inference and unknowns are separate.
- **Work item / issue:** durable scope and criteria, potentially many slices/PRs.
- **Lifecycle:** GitHub issue open/closed; closure alone is not delivery evidence.
- **Project fields:** actual board facts/options interpreted under local policy, not canonical devsys states.
- **Selection / claiming / blocking:** repository conventions composed from explicit primitives;
  native assignments/field writes do not create an atomic reservation.
- **Local slice completion:** reviewed, clean, pushed work, or configured local-only closure;
  not a delivered PR or completed issue.
- **Delivery observation:** current PR/revision facts and assessed named conditions, not proof
  of every local requirement.
- **Reference manifest:** optional versioned per-item evidence references, not a workflow ledger.
- **Guarded completion:** explicit issue closure after configured supported conditions and
  scope confirmation; Project terminal options and unsupported manual conditions remain local.
- **Unknown / not evaluated:** unavailable/ambiguous or unconfigured assessment; never success.
- **Partial/uncertain effect:** confirmed writes distinguished from unknown or missing effects;
  re-read before retry rather than silently rolling back human changes.
